# Changelog

All notable changes to this project. Each entry links to its full per-version file in [changelog/](changelog/).

## [0.2.1](changelog/0.2.x/0.2.1.md) — 2026-11-23 · 🛡️ Security

riksdag_search_members gains committee rosters and a family-name prefix, report-mode votes carry chamberStatus and decisionDate, page ceilings rise to 200 documents and 100 speeches, and searches no longer fail on duplicated speeches or mixed document rows.

## [0.2.0](changelog/0.2.x/0.2.0.md) — 2026-11-15 · ⚠️ Breaking

Pages end at the caller's limit, tools/list is lighter, identifier and date inputs validate at the schema, and the server publishes a sweden-parliament-guide skill. Response-fitting `truncated` fields and member-vote `date` are removed.

## [0.1.2](changelog/0.1.x/0.1.2.md) — 2026-10-25

Parliamentary discovery and complete vote records.

## [0.1.1](changelog/0.1.x/0.1.1.md) — 2026-10-25

Swedish parliamentary records and votes through seven read-only tools.
